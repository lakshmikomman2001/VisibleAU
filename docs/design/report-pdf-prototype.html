The picture, in plain English

When local customers ask AI for help with the kind of work Omega Services does, Omega comes up in about 1 in 6 answers: 5 of the 30 we counted. When it does appear, it's typically listed first, and every mention linked a source.

The engines don't agree. Perplexity mentioned Omega in 3 of 10 answers and ChatGPT in 2 of 10, while Gemini didn't mention it at all. Claude couldn't be measured this time.

AI leans heavily on directories, citing seven of them 36 times, but none of those citations linked Omega's own listings. That is the clearest, most fixable gap in this report (see section 04).

07 · Method and notes

How these numbers were measured

What we asked

13 questions per engine: 10 everyday questions a local customer would ask (they don't name your business) and 3 branded questions. Visibility uses the everyday questions only.

What counts as a mention

Your business is named in the answer and verified against your business details. Similar names that don't match aren't counted.

What's excluded

  • Answers that failed (errors are shown, never counted)
  • Branded questions, from the headline
  • Engines that failed every question, from comparisons over time

Glossary

Visibility
The share of everyday answers that mention you. 17% = 5 of 30.
95% confidence interval
The range the true rate very likely falls in, given how many answers we have. More answers give a narrower range.
Typical position
Your median rank when an answer lists several businesses.
Source cited
How many of your mentions linked to a web page as their source.
Directory citation
An answer that links a directory page (e.g. hipages). "Your profile" means the link goes to your own listing.

Good to know

AI answers change over time and by location. A single audit is a snapshot; regular audits show the direction.

Data residency

Your account data is stored in Australia. This report is stored in Sydney (AWS ap-southeast-2).
