## Ranked actions

**1. Fan-out (#5) — the only strong build-worthy item.**
The standout: a market movement that lines up with something you *already have* and competitors mostly *don't*. Fan-out coverage correlating ~0.77 with citation means it's close to the real mechanic of AI visibility.
- **The work is productisation, not a new engine** — surface and sharpen the existing fan-out sim into something demo-able and trustworthy, a headline selling point.
- **Do it sooner rather than after multi-region** — it upgrades the Phase 2 you're taking to market right now, so it compounds with the go-to-market track, not the build-foundation track.
- Verdict: **clear yes.**

**2. Engine rebalance (#4) — small tuning, do it whenever.**
A config/weighting change (Claude rising), not a project. Mostly this finding just *validates* your multi-engine coverage. Low effort, low urgency.

**3. Positioning edits (#1, #2) — not builds; fold into messaging now.**
- #2 is a sales line for agency conversations today (earned, not bought).
- #1 is an emphasis edit: reweight the llms.txt generator down, don't oversell it — this *protects credibility* with technical agencies who know llms.txt is hype.
- Both are free and immediate.

**4. Agentic shopping (#3) — defer, watch, demand-pull only.**
The signal is real but the market is explicitly *messy* (checkout unsettled). It only matters for the *e-commerce* segment, which isn't your core AU-agency/SMB wedge. Building now = betting effort on a forming channel. Revisit only if agencies with e-commerce clients start asking; scope to discovery, never checkout.
